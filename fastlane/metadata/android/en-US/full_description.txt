Tadbeer (تَدْبِير) is a free, open-source, offline-first Quranic reading and reflection (Tadabbur) app with no ads and no analytics. The internet is used only to stream or download audio recitation.

Key Features:
• Full 604-page Uthmani Mushaf with 5 selectable open-source Quranic fonts (Scheherazade New, Amiri Quran, Uthmanic Hafs, Al-Mushaf, Neirizi).
• Comprehensive offline Quranic Word Meanings lexicon integrated directly into the Mushaf and searchable standalone.
• Reflection (Tadabbur) notes and bookmarks linked to verses, with local JSON backup and restore.
• Customizable local reminders and alarms for daily Quran reading.
• Complete privacy: your data stays on your device, with zero tracking or telemetry.
