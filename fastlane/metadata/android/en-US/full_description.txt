Tadbeer: from reading to reflection. A free, open-source Quran app that works offline, with no account, no ads and no tracking. The internet is used only to stream or download recitation.

Reflection tools:
• Reflection pause: four questions at any ayah that open its meanings, saved in a reflection journal.
• Ayah links: gather ayahs from different surahs into one theme and see them as a network or a list.
• Reflection pathways: 15 themes, with an ayah and a question each day for at least seven days.

Mushaf and listening:
• The 604-page Madinah Mushaf in the Digital Khatt font, or flowing text in five Quran fonts.
• A radial menu for each ayah: reflect, note, bookmark, highlight, share and listen.
• Recitation by 7 reciters, with background playback and downloads for offline listening.

Your journey with the Quran:
• Khatma plans, day streaks, weekly goals, a Mushaf map and a monthly report.
• Memorization with spaced review.

Duas and dhikr:
• 91 duas from the Quran with a qunut filter, plus duas from the Sunnah and the Ahlulbayt, including al-Sahifa al-Sajjadiyya.
• Morning, evening, sleep, waking, after-prayer and mosque adhkar, with a counter for each.
• An istighfar and tasbeeh counter with 49 formulas and auto counting.

Also: a library of booklets you read inside the app, unified search, Qibla direction, reminders, a home-screen widget, encrypted backup, and five themes with light and dark mode, in Arabic and English.

Your data stays on your device. Approximate location is used only for the Qibla and never leaves your device.
